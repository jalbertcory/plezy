This PR records Android player and language changes from [edde746/plezy](https://github.com/edde746/plezy). The destination fork is [jalbertcory/plezy](https://github.com/jalbertcory/plezy). Credential draft history remains in the original `android-first` checkout.

## Android mini player and episode track languages — 2026-10-13


Intent: browse while a video keeps playing and make Japanese audio with English dialogue subtitles easier to select. Status: isolated PR draft on `codex/android-mini-player-languages`, based on the fork’s `main`. Release and merge remain unauthorized.

Baseline: `edde746/plezy`, local `upstream/main`, at `96504b618d99113434029080e52d5e3413ec588d`. No upstream sync occurred for this change.

Approved scope: Android. Phone/tablet playback can minimize with a central downward swipe or the down-chevron button, then restore by tapping or swiping up on the mini player. The mini player has pause/play and close controls. This applies to the built-in VOD player inside the profile navigator. Live TV, external players, Automotive, and Watch Together retain their existing navigation. Android TV and Fire TV receive the language settings, track selection, and episode-card metadata; their remote-oriented playback does not gain swipe minimization. Other platforms retain their existing runtime behavior and await opt-in.

Affected files:

- `lib/navigation/android_video_session.dart`, `profile_session_screen.dart`, `lib/utils/video_player_navigation.dart`, `lib/screens/video_player_screen.dart`, its `parts/build.dart` and `parts/pip.dart`, and `lib/widgets/android_video_mini_player.dart` plus `video_controls/`: keep one keyed player screen across route moves, resize the existing native surface, and clear its thumbnail rectangle through the browsing page. Modal sheets hide the mini player without stopping playback. Closing or replacing playback uses the existing cleanup coordinator. System auto-PiP is disabled while minimized and restored with fullscreen playback. Playback prompts restore fullscreen so they remain actionable.
- `lib/utils/android_track_metadata.dart`, `lib/services/android_track_preferences.dart`, `settings_service.dart`, `track_selection_service.dart`, `plex_client.dart`, `jellyfin_client.dart` and its `parts/playback.dart`, and `lib/screens/settings/playback_settings_screen.dart`: Android language defaults apply after explicit/carried choices and before server fallback, including transcode negotiation. Full-dialogue preference rejects labelled signs-only/forced candidates. If the requested language has only those candidates, the automatic selection is off; manual selection remains available. Semantic episode carry separates labelled dialogue and signs tracks.
- `lib/widgets/android_episode_tracks.dart`, `episode_card.dart`, and `lib/utils/track_label_builder.dart`: episode cards show audio and subtitle languages, subtitle role/title/codec/stream identity, and unknown metadata. Mounted online cards fetch missing item metadata without opening playback. Multiple versions are labelled as an aggregate. Offline cards use supplied metadata. TV horizontal rails retain their existing presentation. New UI text is English; translation remains pending.
- Added Android preference/session/player-screen tests and updated affected settings, episode, gesture, Plex, and Jellyfin tests under `test/`.

Compatibility: new nullable `android_audio_language` and `android_subtitle_language` preferences default to following server settings. `android_prefer_dialogue_subtitles` defaults to true and applies when a subtitle language is configured. These keys participate in the existing settings import/reset registry but are ignored outside Android. No data migration, schema change, new dependency, credential change, or native platform change was introduced. Rollback removes this implementation; older builds ignore the new keys. A title can identify a language only when it provides unambiguous evidence. Untagged tracks remain unknown, and titles cannot prove subtitle content completeness.
